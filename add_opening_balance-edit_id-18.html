Opening balance / 3 checks ✳

Review before replacing.

Updating this record replaces its payment history with one consolidated payment if the paid amount is positive.

  1. Match the supplier & reference

    Choose the company you owe. Leave the reference blank to generate an OB- reference automatically.

  2. Reconcile total and paid

    Enter a positive original total and the amount already paid. Paid cannot be negative or exceed the total. The remainder becomes supplier balance due.

  3. Keep a useful record

    Use the historical date. When an amount is paid, the note is stored with its consolidated payment entry. Review the supplier ledger after saving.
